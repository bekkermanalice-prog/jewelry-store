Care Guide

Keep it as bright as day one

A little routine care keeps gold, silver, and gemstones looking the way they did the day they arrived at your door.

Ask us a care question

01

Store separately

Keep each piece in its own soft pouch to prevent scratching between metals and stones.

02

Last on, first off

Apply perfume and lotion before jewelry, and remove pieces before showering or sleeping.

03

Warm water clean

A soft cloth and lukewarm water with mild soap restores shine — no harsh chemicals needed.

04

Annual check-up

Message us once a year and we'll talk you through a quick at-home clasp and setting check.
